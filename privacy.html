Privacy Policy

Mercan IPTV · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Mercan IPTV ("the app") is developed by Bora Tuncer. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The server address, username and password you enter, as well as your favorites, watch history and settings, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us.

Connections to your provider

To show channels and play videos, the app connects directly to the IPTV server you enter. That server is operated by your own service provider, not by us. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to those connections.

Deleting your data

You can delete an account inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all data it has stored on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
