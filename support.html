About this app
I wrote Mercan IPTV for my son, Mercan, and named it after him. It is a personal project, and the app is completely free: no price, no in-app purchases, no ads and no subscriptions sold by us. You only need the subscription you already have from your own IPTV provider.
Contact us
Questions, problems or feedback? Email bemavision.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Does Mercan IPTV provide channels or content?

No. Mercan IPTV is a player only. It does not provide, sell or include any channels, movies, series or other media. You need an existing subscription from your own IPTV service provider.

How do I add my account?

Open the app, tap Add account and enter the server address, username and password given to you by your provider (Xtream Codes format, for example http://server.com:8080).

A channel or video does not play

Streams come directly from your provider's server. If a stream does not play, check your internet connection and confirm with your provider that the channel is active and your subscription is valid.

Where are my login details stored?

Only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
